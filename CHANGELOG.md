## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-04

First public release.

### Simulation framework (`fpv_analysis`)
- Hourly model chain:
  - over-water microclimate;
  - Spencer/Erbs/Hay–Davies irradiance and a humidity-dependent spectral factor;
  - Faiman module temperature with dew hysteresis;
  - dust and sea-salt soiling with NaCl deliquescence;
  - PVWatts DC and inverter models.
- Module degradation from four mechanisms: intrinsic, Peck temperature–humidity with lagged encapsulant moisture,
  salt-assisted PID, and chloride corrosion. Results include 25-year energy and LCOE.
- Scenarios: coastal land PV, freshwater FPV, saline FPV. Mitigation options can be switched separately: PID-resistant
  (IEC TS 62804-1), salt-mist-hardened (IEC 61701), glass–glass, and cleaning interval.
- Uncertainty analysis:
  - weather ensemble (20 synthetic years);
  - Monte Carlo over 28 parameters, with convergence checked to 5,000 samples;
  - Spearman and Sobol sensitivity.
- Studies: thresholds, mitigation matrix, LCOE break-even and a weather-generator consistency check.
- Verification: 24 unit and regression tests, including code-to-code checks against pvlib.

### Manuscripts
- `paper/`: IEEE Transactions version, revised over three review rounds, with response letters.
- `paper_simpat/`: *Simulation Modelling Practice and Theory* (Elsevier) version, with cover letter and highlights.
- Every number, table and figure is generated by `./reproduce.sh`. Figures are audited for print standards by
  `tools/check_figures.py` (see `FIGURE_QA.md`).

### Known limitations
- Results are model-based scenario predictions. No measured site or FPV field data have been used for validation yet.
- The weather input is a synthetic typical year built from monthly normals.
